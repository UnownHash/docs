# Dragonite Troubleshooting

## Quests not scanning

- Check your **Area** in Dragonite Admin. Make sure:

    - A quest route is saved
    - **Quest Mode** is enabled and has workers
    - A [schedule](/docs/dragonite/scheduler#quests) starts quests for the area (or legacy **Quest Hours** are set)

- Check that you have quest-capable accounts. With [quest quarantine](/docs/dragonite/accounts-auth#quest-quarantine) only PTC accounts that are not AR-banned can quest.
- Check your Dragonite logs: `grep QUESTCHECK` and `grep SCHEDULER`

## Koji route issues

- Check your Dragonite logs: `grep KOJI`
- Check scheduled route jobs in **Dragonite Admin → Scheduler → Run Ledger**, or in your logs: `grep SCHEDULER`
- Check [`/scheduler/diagnostics`](/docs/dragonite/api#scheduler) for schedule configuration problems.

## Dragonite doesn't start

- `rotom endpoint is missing from config`: set `[rotom] endpoint`.
- `[auth.ptc] has no remote_auth_url ...`: set `remote_auth_url`, or keep `token_init_enabled = false` and `token_refresh_only = true`. Without remote auth, refresh tokens can't be collected. See [Accounts & Auth](/docs/dragonite/accounts-auth#remote-auth).
- `invalid accounts required level`: `accounts.required_level` must be between 31 and 40.
